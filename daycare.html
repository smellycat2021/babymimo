What it does

The three jobs that eat your week

💳

Get paid without asking twice

Tuition autopay with saved cards, one-tap “charge all past-due,” per-child payment history, and subsidy tracking. No more chasing families at pickup for last month's tuition.

📋

Compliance without the binder

Digital check-in/out with live staff-to-child ratio alerts against your license limits, weekly menus with CACFP components tracked per meal, staff scheduling, leave, and payroll.

📸

Families see the day, live

Photos, activities, naps, and meals land in each family's own baby-tracking app — not a daily report that disappears. It's a benefit you can put on your tour flyer.
